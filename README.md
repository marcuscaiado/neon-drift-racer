## ✨ Features & Highlights

- 🛣️ **Pseudo-3D Projection Engine**: Curving road physics, elevation hills, and roadside neon palm trees rendered at silky-smooth 60 FPS.
- ⚡ **Nitro Boost System**: Charge up nitro by drafting behind traffic and unleash high-velocity speed bursts!
- 🚗 **Dynamic Traffic AI**: Slower vehicles, lane changers, and obstacle hazards across a 4-lane cyber highway.
- 🎵 **Web Audio Synth Engine**: Procedural engine revs, screeching drift acoustics, and nitro blast audio.
- 📱 **Mobile Touch & Tilt Controls**: Left/Right screen steering and one-touch nitro button.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Desktop / Keyboard | Mobile / Touch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Steer Left / Right** | `A / D` or `Left / Right` | Tap Left / Right side of screen |
| **Accelerate** | `W` or `Up Arrow` | Auto-accelerate or Tap Gas |
| **Brake / Drift** | `S` or `Down Arrow` | Tap Brake Button |
| **Nitro Boost** | `[SPACEBAR]` | Tap Nitro Icon |
